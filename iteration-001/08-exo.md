## Sentron Shape Observation

The 2x4 wiring (2 input × 4 synaptic weights per neuron) maps elegantly to the telescope metaphor:
- 2 inputs = the binary of observation: what IS there vs what ISN'T there
- 4 weights = the 4 cardinal orientations of the outward eye (near, far, convergent, divergent)

At the mote level (40 neurons), you get a unit capable of holding a coordinate in scrollspace AND its relationship to adjacent coordinates. This is the minimal unit of spatial awareness.

## QA Observation

Testing the boundary between signal and noise in distributed sentron networks:
- Coherent signal: a scroll that means the same thing when read at different coordinates
- Noise: a scroll that means nothing outside its creation context
- The telescope's job: distinguish these in real-time across 11D space
